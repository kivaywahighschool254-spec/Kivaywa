Academics at Kivaywa

Senior school under Kenya’s Competency-Based Curriculum (Grades 10 to 12): three pathways, seven subjects, and a first real step towards your career.

Pathways & Subjects

Learners choose one pathway, then pick three elective subjects from its tracks. Electives can be mixed across pathways to match a career goal.

What Every Learner Takes

Each senior school learner studies seven subjects: four compulsory and three chosen to fit their pathway, career interests, aptitude and personality.

  • English
  • Kiswahili or Kenya Sign Language
  • Mathematics: Core Mathematics for STEM, Essential Mathematics for other pathways (non-STEM learners may take Core Mathematics based on junior school results)
  • Community Service Learning (CSL)

Learners who specialise in English or Kiswahili follow a differentiated curriculum in scope, experiences and assessment. Senior school has 40 lessons a week, each 40 minutes long.

How Placement Works

Grade 9 learners choose a pathway, three subject combinations and preferred schools through the national online platform.

  • The KJSEA counts for 60 per cent of the placement score
  • School-based assessments from Grades 7 and 8 count for 20 per cent each
  • Junior school performance in specific learning areas is the main factor for pathway placement
  • Each learner can list up to 12 schools, drawn from four school clusters
  • Not every learner gets their first choice of school or combination

Looking Ahead

The first CBC senior school class began in January 2026 and finishes in 2029. We guide each learner towards a combination that matches their goals, such as a future law student mixing History and Citizenship, Literature in English, and Theatre and Film.
